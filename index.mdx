## How It Works in 3 Steps

ScatterBrain's workflow is straightforward. From URL to PDF in moments:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste Your Atlas Docs URL">
    Copy the root URL of any Atlas documentation site and paste it into the ScatterBrain input field. ScatterBrain will validate the link and detect the full documentation structure automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Export Options (Optional)">
    Optionally adjust export settings such as page order, cover page inclusion, or content filters before generating your file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Your PDF">
    Click the **Export PDF** button and ScatterBrain will crawl every page, render it, and bundle the results into a single downloadable PDF — delivered straight to your browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>
